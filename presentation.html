Industry Analysis

The Payroll & Compliance Crisis in Indian Enterprises

Why traditional spreadsheets and legacy HRMS systems generate legal notices and financial leakage

TEAM T-REX
1. Statutory Penalties
EPFO, ESIC & TDS Audits
  • 42% of Indian startups face statutory notices due to erroneous EPF 12% calculations against wage ceilings.
  • Union Budget FY 2026-27 revised Section 115BAC slabs with standard deduction ₹75,000 causing massive TDS withholding errors.
  • Multi-state Professional Tax (PT) variances lead to severe non-compliance fines.
  • Steep 12% to 24% annual interest penalties for delayed statutory return filings.
2. Financial Leakage
Salary Anomalies & Spikes
  • Negative net salary violations under Section 7 of the Payment of Wages Act (deductions exceeding 50%).
  • Loss of Pay (LOP) disconnect: Unapproved employee absences fail to synchronize with salary calculations.
  • Salary spike anomalies: Duplicate bonuses and unapproved salary increments slip past reviewers.
  • Absence of automated pre-flight validation gates before bank funds release.
3. Operational Chaos
Biometric Fraud & Paper Receipts
  • Buddy punching and proxy manual punch logs in distributed/hybrid workspaces.
  • Expensive on-premise biometric fingerprint hardware requiring physical cabling and repairs.
  • No machine-learning link between employee vision recognition and salary ledger.
  • Fragmented contractor invoices, reimbursement expense claims, and Form 12BB tax proof chaos.
